Professional self-assessment

Maurice Sapp — B.S. Computer Science

Introduction

My name is Maurice Sapp, and I am completing the Bachelor of Science in Computer Science program at Southern New Hampshire University. Throughout this program, I developed technical skills in software engineering, databases, algorithms, security, and communication. Creating this ePortfolio allowed me to reflect on my growth as a computer science student and demonstrate the knowledge I gained through coursework, projects, and internship experiences. As I progressed through the program, I became more interested in Health IT Support and working with systems that help organizations manage and protect important information. This portfolio represents both my technical growth and my professional goals.

Collaborating & communicating with stakeholders

Throughout my coursework, I learned the importance of collaboration and communication when working on technical projects. Completing assignments, participating in discussions, and receiving instructor feedback helped me improve my ability to explain technical concepts to different audiences. My internship experience also helped me develop professional communication skills while working on projects related to accessibility, technology, and organizational support. These experiences taught me that successful technology solutions require both technical knowledge and effective communication with stakeholders, users, and team members.

Data structures & algorithms

The Computer Science program strengthened my understanding of data structures and algorithms. Through the Course Planner artifact, I learned how different data structures affect performance and efficiency. I improved the project by implementing an unordered map to support faster course lookups while still maintaining a vector for ordered output. This enhancement helped me understand trade-offs between different approaches and demonstrated how algorithm selection can improve the performance of software applications. I also strengthened my problem-solving skills by improving validation and preventing duplicate course entries.

Software engineering & databases

My work with the AnimalShelter project helped me build software engineering and database skills. In the software design enhancement, I improved the organization of the code by separating responsibilities into smaller methods, adding validation, improving error handling, and implementing complete CRUD functionality. These changes improved the maintainability and reliability of the application. For the database enhancement, I expanded the MongoDB functionality by strengthening database operations, improving validation, and adding additional safeguards to protect data integrity. These enhancements demonstrated my ability to design, develop, and improve database-driven applications using industry-relevant tools and practices.

Security

Security became an important focus throughout my program. I learned that secure systems require more than simply making software work correctly. Developers must anticipate potential problems, validate user input, protect sensitive information, and design applications that reduce risks. In my enhancements, I incorporated validation checks, improved error handling, and strengthened database interactions to support better security practices. These experiences helped me develop a stronger security mindset that I will continue to build throughout my career.

Portfolio summary

The artifacts included in this ePortfolio work together to demonstrate my growth across multiple areas of computer science. The Software Design and Engineering artifact highlights my ability to improve application structure and maintainability. The Algorithms and Data Structures artifact demonstrates my understanding of efficiency, performance, and problem solving. The Databases artifact showcases my ability to work with data management systems and database operations. Together, these artifacts represent the skills and knowledge I developed throughout the Computer Science program.

Looking ahead

As I move forward after graduation, I plan to continue building my skills in Health IT Support, database management, and healthcare technology systems. My goal is to work in a role that combines technical problem solving with supporting healthcare professionals and organizations. The knowledge and experience I gained throughout this program have prepared me for the next stage of my career and provided a strong foundation for continued professional growth.
